Renter Handbook

Gutted Fitness · The Zone

Everything you need to know as a Gutted Fitness studio renter. Bookmark this page. It stays up to date.

Welcome

Thanks for renting at The Zone. You now have monthly access to the Blue Room and Black Room. Here is how everything works.

Your access

  • Monthly subscription includes access daily from 4:30 AM to 11:00 PM.
  • You will receive a unique door code in your welcome email. Do not share it.
  • Both the Blue Room and Black Room are available to renters. Group class rooms are off limits during class times.
  • Wifi: see the laminated card on the wall by the entrance.

Booking your studio time

Book your slot through the calendar below. Class times are blocked automatically so you cannot accidentally double book over a session.

Open Booking Calendar
  • Minimum booking length: 1 hour.
  • You can book 1, 2, or 3 hour blocks.
  • Cancellations within 2 hours of start are non refundable.

House rules

  • Lock the door when you leave. Confirm the deadbolt.
  • No outside trainers without prior approval from Christie.
  • Bring your own water and small towels. Sanitizing wipes are provided.
  • Music: use the studio speakers or your own headphones. Keep volume reasonable.
  • Children must be supervised at all times. They cannot use the equipment.

Equipment

  • Wipe down equipment after each use using the sanitizing wipes by the entrance.
  • Re rack all weights and return mats to the wall rack.
  • If a piece of equipment is broken or unsafe, do NOT use it. Report it immediately.

Refreshments

  • A small fridge with hydration packs is available. Help yourself. Included with your rental.
  • Protein powder and snacks are stocked weekly. Use a clean scoop.
  • Please dispose of trash in the bin by the entrance, not in the studio.

Reporting issues

  • Equipment break or safety concern: text Christie at the number in your welcome email. Include a photo if possible.
  • Accidental damage: no penalty if reported within 24 hours. Hidden damage discovered later is your responsibility.
  • Lost door code: text Christie. A new code is issued within 24 hours.
  • After hours emergency: call 911 first, then text Christie.

Waiver

All renters must complete a liability waiver before their first use of the studio.

Open Waiver Form

Plan, billing, cancellation

  • Coach's Key is a recurring monthly plan at $350.
  • You can cancel anytime through your Wix member area. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
  • No refunds for partial months.

Feedback and renewal

Have a suggestion, want to extend access, or leave feedback? Reply to your welcome email or text Christie. Renter feedback shapes how this space evolves.
